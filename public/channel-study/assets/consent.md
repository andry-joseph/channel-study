# Consent

**What this is:** a class project for CS 6630 at the University of Utah. It studies how the way a chart is drawn affects the choices people make.

**What you will do:** look at 21 charts and pick one of two options for each. It takes about 5 minutes.

**Voluntary:** you can stop at any time by closing the page. Nothing happens if you stop.

**Privacy:** the survey does not ask for your name, email, or any information that identifies you. Answers are stored without names and used only for this class project.

**Risks:** none beyond everyday computer use.
