# Welcome!

Thank you for helping with my CS 6630 (Visualization) class project at the University of Utah.

You will see **21 short charts**. Each one shows two options (like two laptops). Pick the one **you** would choose.

- There are no wrong answers, except for a few charts that check you are paying attention.
- Go with your first instinct. Each chart should take only a few seconds.
- The whole survey takes about **5 minutes**.
